Make the tab bar work. Tapping a tab (Home, Explore, Saved, Profile) shows its screen:

- The Screens row slides so that screen is showing: x 0, −402, −804 or −1206. It moves quickly without bouncing past its target, and settles within a second.
- The Indicator pill springs under the tab: its x is 22.25, 122.75, 223.25 or 323.75 (y stays 6).
- The current tab's icon shapes and label turn indigo (#5B5CF6) and the other tabs are gray (#A1A1AA). Home starts selected.
