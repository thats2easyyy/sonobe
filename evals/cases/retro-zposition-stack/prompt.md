Make a stack of 3 cards from the Card layer: one layer, three copies. Each copy sits 12 points lower than the one before it (copy 0 at y 300, copy 1 at y 312, copy 2 at y 324, all at x 201), and copy 0, the first one, is drawn on top of the others.

Tapping the top card fades it out (to opacity 0 in about 0.2 seconds). Then the card under it is on top and can be tapped the same way.
