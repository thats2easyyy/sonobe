Make a deck of 3 cards from the Card layer: one layer, three copies stacked in the same spot (x 201, y 420). Copy 0 is the top card and draws in front of the others.

- Only the top card can be dragged. It follows the finger sideways. Let go past 100 points, or flick it faster than 600 points per second, and it's thrown off the screen that way (to x −300 on the left or 700 on the right) and stays there. Otherwise it springs back to the middle.
- Once a card is thrown, the card under it becomes the top card.
- When all three are gone, tapping Reset brings the whole deck back to the middle.
