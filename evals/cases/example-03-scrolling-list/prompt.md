Make the inbox scroll. The Window shows the Content through it (the window is 724 tall, the content 1264).

- Dragging moves the list with the finger. A flick glides, slows down, and stops at the last message (Content y −540). Pulling down past the top stretches, then springs back to 0.
- The header gains a shadow as messages slide under it: its shadow opacity goes from 0 to 0.14 over the first 24 points scrolled, and stays at 0.14 after that.
- Once the list has scrolled more than 400 points, the Back to Top pill slides up from y 940 to y 800 (x stays 201). Tapping it scrolls smoothly back to the top, and the pill slides away again.
